Delete your data

In the app (fastest)

Open KD Video Poster, go to Settings and tap Delete account, then confirm. Straight away that deletes:

  • your account and sign in,
  • every linked TikTok, Instagram, YouTube and Facebook account and its tokens (revoked with the platform where it allows that),
  • your post history,
  • any videos still waiting to be posted.

Posts already published on the platforms stay there. Delete them in each platform's app if you want them gone. If you subscribe, cancel in your Apple ID settings too, or Apple keeps billing you.

Without the app

Email khai.dao.dev@gmail.com from the address you signed in with (or tell me your Apple relay address) and ask for your data to be deleted. I'll do it within 30 days and confirm by email.

Only remove one platform

In the app, go to Accounts and tap Disconnect on that platform. Or remove access from the platform's side:

  • TikTok: Settings and privacy, Security, Manage app permissions.
  • Instagram: Settings, Website permissions, Apps and websites.
  • Facebook: Settings and privacy, Settings, Business integrations.
  • Google and YouTube: myaccount.google.com/permissions.
