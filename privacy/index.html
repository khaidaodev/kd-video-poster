KD Video Poster

Privacy policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

The short version

KD Video Poster posts your videos to the social accounts you link, when you ask it to. It only keeps what it needs to do that. There are no ads, no analytics, no tracking and nothing is sold. It never asks for your social media passwords: you sign in to each platform on the platform's own page.

Who runs it

KD Video Poster is made and run by one independent developer in the UK. You can reach me at khai.dao.dev@gmail.com.

What is stored

  • Your sign in. Your email address, or the private relay address Apple gives if you use Sign in with Apple.
  • Linked accounts. For each account you link: the platform's id for it, its username or display name, its profile picture link, its account type and the access tokens the platform gives us. Tokens are encrypted and the app on your phone never sees them.
  • Your videos, briefly. When you post, the video is uploaded to private storage so it can be sent to each platform. It's deleted as soon as every platform has it, and never kept longer than 24 hours.
  • Your posts. The caption, YouTube title, TikTok settings, which accounts you posted to, and the result from each platform (a link to the post, or an error). This is your history in the app.
  • Your plan. How many free posts you've used, and whether you subscribe.

What is not collected

No analytics, no advertising ids, no location, no contacts, no browsing history, and none of your other photos or videos. The app only sees the video you pick or record. It doesn't read your social feeds, messages, followers or comments.

How it's used

Only to post what you ask, to the accounts you choose, and to show you how it went. Your data is never used for advertising and never sold.

Who else handles it

  • The platforms you post to. TikTok, Instagram, YouTube and Facebook receive your video, caption and settings when you post to them. What they do with it is covered by their own privacy policies.
  • Supabase hosts the sign in, database, storage and server code, in the UK or EU. It processes data only on my behalf.
  • Apple handles payments for subscriptions. I never see your card details. When subscriptions launch, RevenueCat will check subscription status for me.

YouTube and Google

KD Video Poster uses YouTube API Services to upload videos to your channel. By linking YouTube you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's Privacy Policy applies to what Google does with your data.

KD Video Poster's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. It only reads your channel's id, name and picture, and uploads the videos you choose.

You can remove KD Video Poster's access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Removing access on each platform

Disconnect an account in the app under Accounts, and its tokens are deleted straight away (and revoked with the platform where the platform allows it). You can also remove access from the platform's side:

  • TikTok: Settings and privacy, Security, Manage app permissions.
  • Instagram: Settings, Website permissions, Apps and websites.
  • Facebook: Settings and privacy, Settings, Business integrations.
  • Google and YouTube: myaccount.google.com/permissions.

How long it's kept

  • Videos: until every platform has them, 24 hours at most.
  • Linked accounts and tokens: until you disconnect them or delete your account.
  • Post history and your account: until you delete your account.

Deleting everything

In the app, go to Settings and tap Delete account. That deletes your account, linked accounts, tokens, post history and any stored videos straight away, and revokes access with each platform where possible. Posts already on TikTok, Instagram, YouTube or Facebook stay there; delete them on the platform if you want them gone. More on the delete your data page.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask for a copy of your data, ask for it to be corrected or deleted, or object to how it's used. Email khai.dao.dev@gmail.com. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Children

KD Video Poster isn't for anyone under 13, or under the minimum age the platforms set in your country.

Security

Everything travels over encrypted connections. Social tokens are encrypted at rest with a key that's kept apart from the database, and each user can only ever read their own data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes too. Big changes will be mentioned in the app.
