Getting started

Sign in, go to Accounts and connect the platforms you use. Then on the Post tab pick or record a video, write a caption, switch on the accounts you want, and tap Post everywhere.

Instagram says my account isn't supported

Apps can only post to Instagram Business or Creator accounts. Switching is free and takes a minute: in Instagram open your profile, tap the menu, then Account type and tools, then Switch to professional account. Then reconnect Instagram.

Why can't I post to my Facebook profile?

Facebook doesn't let any app post to personal profiles, only to Pages. You can make a Page for free in the Facebook app, then reconnect Facebook.

What's TikTok first?

It sends your video to your TikTok inbox as a draft, so you can add a TikTok sound and post it from TikTok. Everything else posts straight away with the sound inside your video. TikTok library sounds can't be carried to other apps.

Why is my post private?

Until PostOnce finishes each platform's review, some platforms only allow private posts from it. Only you can see them. You can make them public in the platform's own app.

Video limits

  • Up to 50 MB and 3 minutes.
  • Facebook Reels: 3 to 90 seconds.
  • Vertical (9:16) works best everywhere.

If a video is too big, export it at 1080p or make it a bit shorter.

A platform failed

Open the post (from History) and tap Try again on the one that failed. Retrying doesn't use another free post. If it says to reconnect, go to Accounts and reconnect that platform first.

Subscriptions

To cancel, open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions. To get a subscription back on a new phone, tap Restore purchases on the subscribe screen.

Deleting your account

In the app, go to Settings and tap Delete account. See delete your data for what that removes.
